# Now Playing owns its tracking, and Media Session sits at its seam

Now Playing is one module — it owns the tuning, the polling, the per-kind parsing and the announcement — and its whole interface is `tune(station)`, `stop()` and `nowPlaying$`, an observable of `NowPlaying | undefined` where `undefined` means "nothing is tuned" and the Station descriptor means "tuned, no track yet". A raw payload is never a value the module publishes: a payload that yields no track leaves the last value alone, so a payload landing after its tune ended cannot lie — which is the structural fix for a late tick flipping Media Session to `playing` while the player is paused. The per-kind readers stay private behind a registry the module holds (the Station descriptor stays declarative data), and the polling itself reaches the network through one injected transport seam, `poll(source, onPayload) => teardown`, whose contract admits that a callback may arrive after teardown because the ICY path provably can. The alternative — a tracker that polls and emits raw payloads plus a pure parser — was rejected: the payloads have exactly one reader, and deleting a separate parser re-derives its switch inside the tracker rather than concentrating complexity anywhere. Media Session is deliberately *not* in the module: it is one adapter subscribing to `nowPlaying$` (and, later, to playback state), so the browser side effect leaves the interface and the property gets a single owner instead of two writers behind one constructor flag.


> **Amended by ADR-0007** (16 September 2026): the transport below is one attempt (`fetch(source)`, `cancel()`), not a repeating `poll(source, onPayload)`, because the failure policy needed a reader only Now Playing has.
